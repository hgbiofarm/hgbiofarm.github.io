CORE TECHNOLOGY

Agri-Food-Tech, Engineered In-House

Two years of rigorous trial cultivation in Korea's coldest onion belt, combined with IT-based data farming.

INNOVATION 01

Winter-Hardy Onion Seed That Withstands
-30°C and Below

Cheorwon and Hwacheon in Gangwon Province are among Korea's coldest farming regions, where winter temperatures drop below -30°C. After successfully completing two years of trial cultivation, Hangyeol Biofarm worked with the Gangwon Agricultural Research & Extension Services and local agricultural technology centers to establish a commercial growing system for high-quality onions that endure these extremes.

  • Cultivars adapted to -30°C winters deliver high overwintering survival rates
  • Firm flesh and outstanding storability cut spoilage dramatically
  • Higher yield per unit area with maximized functional active compounds
INNOVATION 02

Standard Cultivation Protocols &
Web-Based Production Data Management

Breaking away from the inconsistent practices of conventional farming, we standardized the entire cycle - sowing, nursery, transplanting, growth management, pest control and harvest - into scientific cultivation protocols. Mobile farm logs digitalize every production record and guarantee uniform quality.

  • Real-time mobile records of growth stages and inputs for every farm
  • Accumulated big data for precise forecasting of volume and shipping windows
  • Flawless traceability that meets corporate buyers' requirements
SMART INFRASTRUCTURE

A 2,000-Pyeong Cold-Storage APC in Cheorwon-gun

Cold storage is not simply warehousing. It is the core infrastructure that links production with distribution, buffers price swings and makes 365-day planned shipments possible.

INFRA 01

2,000-Pyeong Smart Cold Storage

Using farmland in Cheorwon-gun, we are building a state-of-the-art cold-chain logistics base that connects receiving, low-temperature aging, processing and shipping in one stop (groundbreaking scheduled for November 2026).

INFRA 02

High-Precision Optical Sorter (KRW 635 Million)

We are introducing an advanced grading system that automatically and precisely judges weight, size, appearance and internal defects - supplying buyer-specific grades and maximizing B2B confidence.

INFRA 03

Full Purchase & Planned Shipments

Instead of produce being dumped at harvest-time lows, everything is stored under stable low temperatures and supplied at a uniform year-round price, exactly when food companies and catering operators need it.
